Obsidian Backup

A personal backup setup for an Obsidian knowledge base.

This private-use setup uses restic to encrypt and version local backups, and rclone to transfer the encrypted backup data to the owner's own Google Drive account.

Google Drive access is requested through Google's OAuth consent process. The account owner grants access and can revoke it in Google Account settings.

This website is informational only. It does not contain or publish the owner's notes, conversations, encryption password, Google Drive files, or authentication credentials.
