Privacy Policy

Obsidian Backup is a personal, single-user backup setup. It is not a public service.

Google Drive access

The owner uses rclone to access their own Google Drive through Google OAuth. That access is used only for storing, listing, retrieving, and managing backup data at the owner's direction.

Backup contents and encryption

restic encrypts Obsidian backup data on the owner's computer before it is transferred to Google Drive. The encryption password is not published by this website or included in the backup.

Handling of data

This website does not request logins, run analytics, use tracking cookies, or collect submitted personal information. The backup tools run locally on the owner's computer. Backup data is stored in the owner's Google Drive account, not on this website. Data is not sold or used for advertising.

Control and revocation

The account owner controls the backups and can delete them from Google Drive. Google OAuth access can be revoked through Google Account connections.

Google Drive's own processing is governed by Google's terms and privacy policy.
